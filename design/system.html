Qbit Nutrition design system

A proposed starter system. Only the brand colors (#04070D, #2D7FFF, #35E0FF, #3DFF8B, Outfit, the blue-to-cyan-to-green wordmark) and the three light-theme variants (#1C5FD6, #08687A, #0B7536) come from the owner. Every other value is a proposal. src/ui/tokens.ts holds a superseded direction and is intentionally not synced. There is no logo asset here, so the app uses a text wordmark with "by qbitdev".

Proposed starter, not project tokens

Dark theme (default)

Glow rule

Dark theme only, primary action and ring arc only. Never cyan or green.

Motion

Press feedback within 100 ms; screens 480 ms; the one orchestrated moment is the 1 s fill of numbers and rings. Reduced motion removes travel and scaling: screens cross-fade in 120 ms, values jump to their target.

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the dots jump instead of travelling.
--qn-dur-press · 100ms with --qn-ease-out · Press feedback (scale on touch). Must land under 100 ms of perceived response.
--qn-dur-fast · 180ms with --qn-ease-out · Color, chip, check mark, small state changes.
--qn-dur-base · 320ms with --qn-ease-out · Item entrance, toast, dialog fade, stepper bump, segmented thumb.
--qn-dur-slow · 480ms with --qn-ease-out · Screen transitions and sheets.
--qn-dur-ring · 1000ms with --qn-ease-out · Calorie number and ring, gauge, macro bars filling to their value.
--qn-ease · cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1) · Standard: color, fades, small moves.
--qn-ease-out · cubic-bezier(.05, .7, .1, 1) · Entrances, screens, fills (fast start, soft landing).
--qn-ease-in · cubic-bezier(.3, 0, .8, .15) · Exits only; rarely needed.
--qn-ease-spring · cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1) · Confirmations: stepper bump, save check, toast, segmented thumb.
